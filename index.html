Independently verified | Global | 4 min read

Two of the industry's newest frontier models are now running neck-and-neck on one of the most closely watched benchmarks for real-world coding ability. Independent testing group Artificial Analysis found GPT-5.6 Sol and Grok 4.5 within a fraction of a point of each other, with Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 close behind.

The benchmark evaluates how well each model handles multi-step engineering tasks rather than isolated puzzle-style questions, using an agentic testing setup where the model plans, edits, and verifies its own work across a full context window before submitting an answer.

Company-reported numbers from each lab's own announcement had suggested a clearer leader — a reminder that self-reported scores and independently reproduced ones don't always agree, and it's worth checking which kind you're looking at before drawing conclusions.

Understand This Story

Benchmark
A standardized test used to compare how different AI models perform on the same tasks, so results can be measured against each other fairly.
Agentic
Describes an AI system that can take multiple steps on its own — planning, acting, and checking its work — rather than just answering one question at a time.
Context window
The amount of text or information an AI model can "see" and consider at once when working on a task.
